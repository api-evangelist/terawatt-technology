# TeraWatt Technology

> TeraWatt Technology Inc. is a next-generation lithium-ion battery company founded in 2020 by
> Ken Ogata and Yifan Tang, with headquarters in California, an R&D center in Santa Clara and
> operations in Japan (Tokyo and a mass-production facility in Shizuoka). It develops, manufactures
> and commercializes lithium-ion cells with ultra-high energy density, high power output and
> improved safety for electric vehicles, drones, eVTOL aircraft and energy-storage systems.
